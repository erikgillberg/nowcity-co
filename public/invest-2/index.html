01Why Now

The built environment is being digitized, and capital has noticed

Places that were designed once and operated reactively are becoming modeled, fabricated, instrumented, and continuously measured. In July 2026 a physical-AI company raised $1.7B led by Andreessen Horowitz† to digitize and operate the physical world, and real-asset allocators are hunting the same exposure. Districts are where physical AI becomes investable: the demand, the deployment environment, and the operating data, in one place, with one owner.

Physical AI makes the company more scalable, measurable, intelligent, and strategically valuable. It does not make it worth doing; the mission does that.
02Why We Exist

Accelerate the transition to regenerative, resilient, and equitable communities

Large pools of capital are moving into real-world assets, with mandates that ask for resilient, sustainable, community-positive holdings that perform for decades. On the ground, renters, buyers, and commercial tenants increasingly choose, and pay more for, places that deliver health, community, and durability. People pay for outcomes. Conventional development, divided among separate teams optimizing separate transactions, cannot deliver them. Now City is built to deliver those outcomes as one product, and to hold them long enough to collect their value.

Regeneration

Restore natural systems, clean air, water, soil, and food, improving human health while rebuilding ecological function at neighborhood and district scale.

Resilience

Homes, mobility, energy, food, and social systems designed to perform under physical and economic stress, strengthening household security and community stability.

Equity

Design around the realities of caregiving, childhood, aging, access, and mobility independence, creating more stable, connected, and inclusive communities for all.

06Business Model

Five mutually reinforcing economic engines

Real development creates the control, proof, demand, data, and cash flow that a scalable platform requires. The corporate case does not depend on Now City becoming a huge developer alone; it depends on these engines compounding.

Engine 1
Owned & JV districts
Master-development fees, development fees, GP and promote economics, land and entitlement value creation, co-investment appreciation, and long-term asset ownership.
Engine 2
Now City Homes
Product and design-system economics, catalog licensing, procurement and supply-chain economics, factory framework agreements, development margin, and demand aggregation across owned and partner projects.
Engine 3
Partner districts & Now City Labs
Paid diagnostics, strategic advisory, development management, owner representation, co-development, success fees and GP participation, and systems and capital structuring, on the four-phase engagement model from carrying capacity through execution and stewardship.
Engine 4
nowcity.ai & the operating systems
Software subscriptions, enterprise licenses, deal-team collaboration, managed data and analytics, digital twins, performance monitoring, benchmarking, and AI-assisted underwriting and operations.
Engine 5
Infrastructure & operating companies
Selective participation in district energy, water and resource recovery, mobility, food and markets, wellness, digital infrastructure, and community services, owned, co-owned, procured, or integrated where repeated demand and durable cash flow justify it.

The engines switch on in sequence

Development leads. Each later engine is unlocked by the proof, demand, and data the earlier ones create, so the revenue base diversifies as the platform matures.

The compounding flywheel

Each district earns the control, proof, demand, data, and cash flow that make the next one faster, cheaper, and more defensible.

External master developers adopt pieces of the platform, expanding demand and data without Now City owning every acre. With each turn, the network of rights, relationships, standards, data, and software becomes harder to reproduce.

What compounds into the moat

  • Master-developer rights, GP positions, and the district pipeline
  • Landowner, city, university, and institutional relationships
  • Now City Homes: the catalog, the order book, the factory framework
  • The three operating systems and their longitudinal district data
  • District standards, procurement specs, and the qualified vendor bench
  • Recurring software and operating contracts; selective OpCo stakes
  • A trusted community brand, and the route from technology to real neighborhoods

Why strategic capital cares

A strategic investor gains distribution into real projects, product validation in controlled deployment environments, operating data across the full lifecycle, and a relationship with a platform that is hard to reproduce. The goal is to own valuable overlap with the majors, on our terms.

Every item in the left column deepens with each district. That is the moat: a compounding set of rights, relationships, standards, and data that no single acquisition or copycat can assemble quickly.

10The Raise

Three investable layers, and a 24-month value-creation plan

Now City combines the scarce, local work of district formation with the scalable economics of homes, software, services, procurement, infrastructure, and operations. Investors participate at the layer that matches their mandate. Platform capital scales a multi-district business; project capital is ring-fenced to defined assets and phases.

Layer 1 · Platform equity

The company that repeats

Ownership of Now City Inc: the developer-operator platform, Now City Homes, the three operating systems, the data rights, GP economics, and every district the platform delivers or helps deliver.

Layer 2 · District & project vehicles

Ring-fenced to the asset

Defined district and phase vehicles, such as the Edgewater district fund, capitalized against a specific value-creation arc with its own exit windows. Documented in the Edgewater data room.

Layer 3 · Infrastructure & OpCos

Utility-like cash flow

Selective operating companies and infrastructure, from district energy and mobility to activation businesses, structured with specialist partners where durable cash flow and control justify ownership.
