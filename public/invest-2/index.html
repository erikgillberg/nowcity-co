01Why We Exist

Accelerate the transition to regenerative, resilient, and equitable communities

Now City is a mission-driven developer and operator. We treat a district as a living, coordinated system and steward it over decades. The mission is not decoration; it is the category, and it is what separates Now City from a generic proptech, robotics, modular-housing, or smart-city company.

Regeneration

Restore natural systems, clean air, water, soil, and food, improving human health while rebuilding ecological function at neighborhood and district scale.

Resilience

Homes, mobility, energy, food, and social systems designed to perform under physical and economic stress, strengthening household security and community stability.

Equity

Design around the realities of caregiving, childhood, aging, access, and mobility independence, creating more stable, connected, and inclusive communities for all.

The human outcome is everyday vitality: complete places where housing, work, food, wellness, mobility, nature, culture, and community come together in one connected daily life. Intelligent systems are useful to the extent that they improve human life, ecological performance, affordability, resilience, and long-term value. Physical AI belongs below the mission, in service of it.
02The Category

Fragmented development cannot deliver an integrated district

Conventional development divides land, buildings, infrastructure, capital, and operations among separate teams and optimizes each transaction in isolation. Urbanism is the opposite discipline: the integration of streets, blocks, buildings, landscape, mobility, energy, water, governance, and culture into a place that continues getting better over time. Now City holds the district as one long-duration product, and that is the category.

Human-first places with intelligent, adaptable physical systems.

Why now: the built environment is being digitized

A major market transition is under way. It is a tailwind for the company we already are, not a new identity.

The old built environment

  • Designed once, financed in silos, delivered by fragmented teams.
  • Operated reactively, poorly instrumented, data lost between phases.
  • Buildings optimized separately from neighborhoods.
  • Little learning transferred from one district to the next.

The emerging built environment

  • Digitally modeled before construction, coordinated across buildings and infrastructure.
  • Increasingly fabricated and assembled, then instrumented during operation.
  • Capable of prediction and adaptation, served by autonomous systems.
  • Continuously measured against financial, human, and ecological outcomes, and improved through portfolio-wide learning.
The thesis is being validated at scale: in July 2026 a physical-AI company raised $1.7B led by Andreessen Horowitz† to digitize and operate the physical world, an independent bet that land, construction, energy, and cross-stack competence are where this economy is won. Now City brings the physical environment, the development rights, the users, the capital, the operating systems, and the place-based purpose together. That is a more powerful role than a deployment layer for any one technology company, and it leaves Now City free to integrate the best systems, build its own, or own the ones that earn it.

04The Now City Stack

Four integrated delivery layers

The Stack is how the physical place gets delivered. Four layers, engineered together from the start rather than optimized in isolation, are what make a Now City district financeable and coherent.

Regenerative placemaking

Walkability, public realm, culture, civic gathering, local business, community programming, and daily life.

Green infrastructure

Energy, water, mobility, food, ecology, landscape, waste, digital connectivity, and resilience at district scale.

Industrialized construction

Standardized systems, factory fabrication, healthy materials, mass timber, kit-of-parts assembly, faster schedules, and higher quality.

Innovative finance

Capital structures that align long-term investors, landowners, public partners, operators, residents, and communities.

06Business Model

Five mutually reinforcing economic engines

Real development creates the control, proof, demand, data, and cash flow that a scalable platform requires. The corporate case does not depend on Now City becoming a huge developer alone; it depends on these engines compounding.

Engine 1
Owned & JV districts
Master-development fees, development fees, GP and promote economics, land and entitlement value creation, co-investment appreciation, and long-term asset ownership.
Engine 2
Now City Homes
Product and design-system economics, catalog licensing, procurement and supply-chain economics, factory framework agreements, development margin, and demand aggregation across owned and partner projects.
Engine 3
Partner districts & Now City Labs
Paid diagnostics, strategic advisory, development management, owner representation, co-development, success fees and GP participation, and systems and capital structuring, on the four-phase engagement model from carrying capacity through execution and stewardship.
Engine 4
nowcity.ai & the operating systems
Software subscriptions, enterprise licenses, deal-team collaboration, managed data and analytics, digital twins, performance monitoring, benchmarking, and AI-assisted underwriting and operations.
Engine 5
Infrastructure & operating companies
Selective participation in district energy, water and resource recovery, mobility, food and markets, wellness, digital infrastructure, and community services, owned, co-owned, procured, or integrated where repeated demand and durable cash flow justify it.

The engines switch on in sequence

Development leads. Each later engine is unlocked by the proof, demand, and data the earlier ones create, so the revenue base diversifies as the platform matures.

The compounding flywheel

Each district earns the control, proof, demand, data, and cash flow that make the next one faster, cheaper, and more defensible.

External master developers adopt pieces of the platform, expanding demand and data without Now City owning every acre. With each turn, the network of rights, relationships, standards, data, and software becomes harder to reproduce.

What Now City owns over time

  • Master-developer rights and GP positions, and a pipeline of district opportunities.
  • Landowner, city, university, and institutional relationships.
  • Now City Homes and its aggregated order book, plus a code-tested, performance-engineered catalog.
  • Development, Deployment, and Neighborhood OS, and longitudinal development and operating data.
  • District standards, procurement specifications, and a qualified ecosystem of builders and technology providers.
  • Recurring software and managed-services contracts, and selective infrastructure and OpCo participation.
  • A trusted consumer and community brand, and a route from physical technology to actual neighborhoods and customers.

Why corporate investment is logical

A strategic investor gains one or more of four rationales: distribution (access to real projects and a multi-district customer channel), product-market validation (controlled environments to specify, buy, deploy, measure, and improve products), data and operating intelligence across the full lifecycle, and future control (a relationship, information rights, commercial access, and a possible path to acquire a hard-to-reproduce platform later).

The acquisition logic is strongest once Now City has accomplished some of a major's ambitions independently. The goal is not to avoid overlap; it is to own valuable overlap, on our terms.

10The Raise

Three investable layers, and a 24-month value-creation plan

Now City combines the scarce, local work of district formation with the scalable economics of homes, software, services, procurement, infrastructure, and operations. Investors participate at the layer that matches their mandate. Platform capital scales a multi-district business; project capital is ring-fenced to defined assets and phases.

Layer 1 · Platform equity

The company that repeats

Ownership of Now City Inc: the developer-operator platform, Now City Homes, the three operating systems, the data rights, GP economics, and every district the platform delivers or helps deliver.

Layer 2 · District & project vehicles

Ring-fenced to the asset

Defined district and phase vehicles, such as the Edgewater district fund, capitalized against a specific value-creation arc with its own exit windows. Documented in the Edgewater data room.

Layer 3 · Infrastructure & OpCos

Utility-like cash flow

Selective operating companies and infrastructure, from district energy and mobility to activation businesses, structured with specialist partners where durable cash flow and control justify ownership.
