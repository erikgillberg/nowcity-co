Milestones

What the money proves, quarter by quarter

The platform's job over the next 18 months is to convert this raise into de-risked, compounding positions. The ladder below is how we will measure ourselves, and how you should measure us.

Months 0-6Lock the foundations
  • Campaign momentum: platform round closed or substantially subscribed; District Fund raising in parallel with the co-GP process under way.
  • Edgewater capital stack in motion: Opportunity Zone nomination window worked, HUD lender interviews complete with an indicative letter, C-PACE and URA diligence closed. Tracked publicly on the capital strategy verification register.
  • Predevelopment facility: CDFI or PRI term sheet that stretches platform dollars with mission-priced credit.
Months 6-12Convert control into value
  • Edgewater entitlement advancing: master plan and engineering moving with the District Fund's capital, district TIF concept submitted to the Urban Renewal Agency.
  • District two shortlist: two to three candidate corridors evaluated against the platform's site criteria, using the underwriting engine, with one advancing to relationship-building.
  • Product externalization: Upside Explorer in the hands of first external deal teams via nowcity.ai, converting the internal engine into commercial validation.
Months 12-18Switch on platform economics
  • Land close in sight: the ~$34M Land SPV assembled, with the landowner's $5M contribution committed under the executed LOI and outside equity organized.
  • Phase 1 capitalization assembled: HUD application in processing, C-PACE committed, TIF framework agreed in principle, OZ equity structured.
  • Recurring platform revenue line: development and asset management fee agreements in place as district capital closes, moving the platform from raise-funded to fee-supported operations.
The pattern to notice: platform dollars are small, but they sit at the control point. $3M of platform capital directs $12M of district value creation, which organizes ~$34M of land capital, which unlocks a $650M development pipeline built on a blended-cost stack. Ownership of the control point is what this raise sells.

Honest Questions

The questions we would ask before writing this check

We would rather earn trust than manage impressions. Direct answers, with the risk we are still working named alongside each.

Concentration

“Is this just a bet on one project in Salem?”

Today, Edgewater is the platform's main engine, and this raise is sized to its milestones. The position you buy is broader: the software has its own commercial path, the second district search is funded in this budget, and the playbook is the durable asset. The risk we are working: near-term platform value does concentrate in Edgewater's progress; the milestone ladder above is designed so that concentration converts into proof quickly.

Stage

“Why equity in a young company instead of the district fund?”

Different jobs. The fund buys a defined value-creation arc with an exit window at land close. Platform equity buys the repeating engine: GP economics, software, and every future district, with more time and more variance. Many partners take both; the campaign is designed so the two reinforce each other. The risk we are working: platform liquidity depends on platform value events; we sequence them deliberately, and the co-GP admission is the first.

Execution

“What if Edgewater slows?”

The district is phased so capital commits against de-risked stages, and the platform's costs stay light by design: partners and coalitions carry execution weight, and mission-priced credit stretches runway. A slower Edgewater delays fees; the entitlement work, the software, and the site criteria keep building value through it. The risk we are working: timeline risk is real in entitlement work everywhere; phasing and the blended-cost stack are the defense.

Revenue

“When does the platform earn money?”

Platform fee agreements switch on as district capital closes: structuring and development fees at land close and Phase 1 capitalization, asset management fees as vehicles operate, promote at stabilization events. Software revenue begins as nowcity.ai reaches external deal teams. The risk we are working: fee timing follows capital timing; the 18-month plan is built to reach fee-supported operations inside this raise's runway.
