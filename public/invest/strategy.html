The Role

Master developer: the actor who holds the whole

A district needs someone to assemble and entitle the land, master-plan the whole, deliver the shared infrastructure and public realm, set the standards every building lives by, and stay for the decade it takes. That actor creates most of the value and carries a structurally advantaged position: it buys land at fragmented-corridor prices and delivers entitled, infrastructure-served pads into a plan it controls. Now City takes this role, holds phases long-term as owner-operator, and keeps the flexibility on any parcel to build, joint-venture, or bring in a best-in-class partner under the master plan.

Most development companies are organized around a building. Now City is organized around a place: capital, design, construction, and operations engineered together from the start, on a horizon measured in decades. That organization is the product, and it is what this raise capitalizes.
The Business Model

Four earning layers, one integrated position

Each layer has its own margin, timing, and risk. Together they mean the platform earns from the first entitlement to the tenth year of operations, and platform investors participate in all of them, on every district.

Layer 1

Land value creation

Assembly, entitlement, and master planning turn fragmented parcels into an investable district. The step change in land value accrues to the positions the platform structures and manages.

Layer 2

Pad monetization

Finished, infrastructure-served parcels are held, joint-ventured, or delivered with phase partners, converting entitlement work into proceeds and aligned partnerships.

Layer 3

Development margin

On phases Now City builds itself: development fees during delivery plus promote at stabilization, underwritten live in the Upside Explorer rather than asserted in a static deck.

Layer 4

Long-term yield

The stabilized district is held as owner-operator and recapitalized rather than sold, compounding value and keeping the platform in its districts for the long term.

Across all four layers, the platform entity earns the GP economics: development and asset management fees, carried interest, and the appreciation of its co-investment positions. As districts multiply, those streams diversify from one project's timeline into a portfolio's.

The Moat

Systems the platform owns

The role is defensible because the systems behind it are hard to assemble: each one is real today, in use on Edgewater, and built to transfer to every district that follows.

Software · nowcity.ai

AI-native underwriting

The Now City Upside Explorer is collaborative underwriting software: live models the whole team can drive together, human judgment plus AI agents, every number traced to its source. On a sample project it walks from an honest negative base case to institutional returns, one named lever at a time. It wins arguments with cities, lenders, and LPs that decks lose, and it is a product with its own commercial path.

Building product

Industrialized, regenerative buildings

Point Access Block design at 93% floor-plate efficiency, Passive House performance by default, industrialized construction that cuts cost, schedule, waste, and carbon. Efficiency is what makes attainable rents pencil; the deep energy scope also maximizes green financing eligibility, from C-PACE basis to agency pricing incentives.

Capital engineering

The blended-cost playbook

A documented method for stacking public value capture, government credit enhancement, tax-advantaged structures, and mission-priced capital into a cost of capital well below conventional finance. Written as a live strategy with a verification register on the Edgewater data room, and repeatable by design.

Method · trust

Coalition development

Great districts are built by coalitions, and coalition-building is a discipline: precedent research, named-door recruiting maps, and public partnership sequenced before vertical capital is at risk. This is slow to copy because it is earned, and it is the platform's distribution channel for both districts and capital.

Where We Play

The mid-sized West, one district at a time

Now City focuses on the West Coast, from the Pacific Northwest to Baja California, where land use, climate, and policy momentum align. Salem is the beachhead: a state capital at the front of a migration wave, with the first district already moving. The platform's site criteria are explicit, and this raise funds evaluation of the next district against them:

  • A mid-sized city with in-migration, supply constraint, and attainability stress
  • An underused corridor already rezoned, or politically ready, for dense mixed use
  • Public tools in place: urban renewal or TIF capacity, incentive programs, OZ position
  • An anchor-capable setting: riverfront, university adjacency, or an employment cluster
  • A landowner situation that rewards patient assembly over auction pricing
  • A civic coalition ready to be organized around a catalytic project
How others did itSt. Louis's Cortex turned $29M of patient anchor capital into 200 controlled acres and a $167M public financing package. Portland's Pearl District paired a private master developer with a city compact that funded streetcar and parks from increment. Newlab at the Brooklyn Navy Yard turned one restored building into an innovation platform spanning cities. Each is one mechanism of the Now City model; the platform's design is to run them together, repeatedly.
