We built a product for this. The Upside Explorer pairs expert human judgment with a team of AI agents on one live, collaborative pro forma: the math is fixed and runs the same way every time, the agents only propose assumptions, and every number traces to its source. We use it to unlock hidden value, compress timelines, and create better outcomes for complex mixed-use projects like Edgewater.
