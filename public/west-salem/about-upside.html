The tool we built, as it opens when you launch: the live Edgewater Phase One pro forma at the honest floor, with the levers, scenarios, and agent fleet ready to work.

No tool on the market could underwrite a district the way we needed: transparently, collaboratively, and honestly enough to hand to an investor. So Now City designed and engineered its own. The Upside Explorer pairs our team’s judgment with a fleet of AI agents on one live pro forma: the math is fixed and runs the same way every time, the agents only propose assumptions, and every number traces to its source. Edgewater is underwritten in it, and the same platform will underwrite every district we build.

Try it and tell us what you think. Play with the Edgewater Phase One pro forma assumptions: change the building type, the costs, the capital stack, and watch the district answer.
