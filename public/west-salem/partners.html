The Model

A master developer is only as strong as its ecosystem

Now City holds the vision, the land strategy, the master plan, and the performance standards. The district itself is delivered with others: specialist developers, capital advisors, designers, builders, and operators, each engaged when the work calls for it. That makes the surrounding ecosystem central to how a district like this gets built well. This page maps that ecosystem and profiles a set of firms whose work we admire and consider representative of the region’s development, capital, and operating talent. We share them to illustrate the caliber of collaborator Edgewater would seek and the range of roles a district of this scale involves. The firms named here are illustrative examples, not an exhaustive or exclusive list. Their inclusion reflects our respect for their work and reputation; it does not imply any existing relationship, agreement, discussion, or endorsement on their part.

Land & Entitlement

Assembly, master planning, zoning, and the public approvals that make 22 acres shovel-ready.

Capital

Platform capital, construction debt, preferred and JV equity, public tools, and recapitalizations at Y4, Y6, and Y8.

Design & Delivery

Master plan and phase architecture, industrialized construction, and the infrastructure and public realm.

Operations & Activation

Interim OpCos, lease-up, property and asset management, retail curation, and district programming that compounds value.

Representative Firms

Firms whose work we admire

The firms below are examples of the exceptional development, management, and capital talent in Oregon and the Pacific Northwest. We highlight them because their work, values, and track records reflect what Edgewater aims to be, and because a district with four phases, several activation layers, and multiple capital events over a decade benefits from collaborators of this caliber. Everything here is drawn from their own public materials. They are illustrative examples, not an exhaustive list, and, to be clear, inclusion implies no relationship, discussion, or commitment on their part. The district’s delivery teams are profiled separately on the Delivery Teams page.

Full-stack real estate platform · Portland, OR

Ethos Companies

ethoscompanies.com · Development, property and asset management, and capital markets

Ethos is a full-stack real estate operation whose three firms span development, operations, and capital markets, independently or together. Ethos Development is a values-based multifamily developer and investor working across ground-up and value-add, with affordable projects in its portfolio, and a 1% for the Planet member whose stated focus on human-centered design, affordability, energy efficiency, and density closely echoes Edgewater’s own aims. Meritus Property Group has managed residential and commercial assets since 1991, from historic restorations to new lease-ups across the Portland and Seattle metros. Ethos Commercial Advisors is a brokerage and capital firm covering debt and equity placement, investment sales, and tenant and landlord representation, licensed in Oregon, Washington, and Texas. A platform that pairs mission-aligned development with in-house operations and capital-markets depth exemplifies the kind of capability a phased, mixed-use district relies on.

Development & entitlement management · Lake Oswego, OR

Macree Companies

macreeco.com · Development leadership, entitlement, and owner’s representation · $3B+ project experience

Macree offers turnkey development leadership from early concept to finished project: development and entitlement management, feasibility and financial analytics, owner’s representation through construction, construction-defect resolution, and joint-venture partnership as principal. Its founder has designed, entitled, and constructed over 5,000 multifamily units, 350,000 SF of retail, and 1.7M SF of office across California and the Pacific Northwest. The firm’s stated focus is complex parcels where context, entitlement insight, and design unlock value a spreadsheet alone would miss, precisely the kind of connective discipline a district assembly like Edgewater involves.

Capital advisory · National · $2B+ closed

Ludian Advisors

ludianadvisors.com · Debt, structured, and JV/Co-GP equity · Transactions $20M to $200M+

An award-winning capital advisory that structures as well as sources: senior debt, subordinate financing, JV and Co-GP equity through institutional and family-office relationships, and credit-enhancement advisory. Its founder’s background spans KPMG, the Quantum Fund, and AIG Global Real Estate, and the firm’s team adds long-tenured lending and institutional asset-management depth. Its closed work includes complex mixed-use construction financings comparable in structure to a phased district. Capital advisory of this depth is what district-scale, long-horizon projects like Edgewater rely on across their financing life.

Second-generation Oregon developer · Portland, OR · Est. 1974

Rembold

rembold.com · Diversified housing, mixed-use, and senior living · $1B+ developed

Rembold is a second-generation Oregon development company with 50 years of building history: 3,400+ houses and lots, 3,300+ apartment and condominium units, and substantial office, industrial, and retail. Two capabilities stand out beyond the headline numbers. Rembold builds and operates senior living as a repeat product line (The Ackerly communities), giving it depth in wellness-oriented, age-inclusive housing. And The Ackerly at Reed’s Crossing was delivered inside another sponsor’s master-planned community, meaning Rembold has experience building well within a master plan held by others, the same structure Edgewater uses: a master developer holding the plan while a proven vertical developer delivers a parcel. A diversified, multi-generational Oregon developer of this caliber is the kind of collaborator a wellness-oriented, mixed-use district would be fortunate to work with.

Sourcing & Onboarding

How we find them and bring them in

We build the surrounding ecosystem the way a district like this rewards: through introductions, industry forums, and small, paid, expandable engagements that let both sides get to know each other before any commitment.

Warm Paths First

Introductions matter. Our advisors open doors into the region’s design, planning, and community-capital communities, and the strongest regional firms carry deep networks whose referrals arrive pre-vetted.

Live Forums

International Mass Timber Conference (Portland, March) for delivery partners; ULI Northwest and NAIOP Oregon for developers and capital; SEDCOR and Salem Chamber for regional anchors; CNU for design; public-market gatherings for the market master search.

Engagement Mechanics

Paid discovery scopes for professional firms; activation OpCos as live auditions for operators; competitive preconstruction for builders; an RFQ for phase development partners once entitlement is visible; the five-move coalition sequence for innovation anchors; lane letters keeping overlapping partners complementary.

Collaborator Convening

As collaborators come aboard, we intend to convene them regularly so everyone can see the whole district, spot gaps, and contribute beyond a single engagement.
