Potential teammates and partners for the Edgewater district, and the value each brings to the vision.

Purpose

Building the bench

Now City leads Edgewater as master developer: setting the vision, holding the district plan, and orchestrating capital, design, and delivery across phases. That model works because execution flows through experienced partners. This page gathers the profiles and capabilities of firms we are evaluating as teammates, and maps where each could add the most value as the district advances. It is an internal working document, unlinked from site navigation, and will evolve as relationships develop.

Development Partner · Portland, OR

Rembold

rembold.com · Founded 1974 · Principals: Kira Cador (President) and Kali Bader

Rembold is a second-generation Oregon development company with 50 years of building history. Founded by Wayne Rembold as a home-building company and now directed by his daughters, the firm has developed over $1 billion of real estate: 3,400+ houses and lots, 3,300+ apartment and condominium units, 580k+ SF of office, 1.8M SF of industrial, and 1.0M SF of retail. The firm pairs entrepreneurial intuition with detailed financial analysis, and operates on five stated convictions: determination, respect, integrity, creativity, and profitability.

Capabilities

Full-cycle development across housing, multifamily, office, industrial, and retail. Deep Oregon market knowledge, long-standing lender and contractor relationships, and a track record of proven economic performance across market cycles.

Potential Value to Edgewater

A credible, multigenerational Oregon sponsor as phase development partner or co-GP. Rembold's mixed-portfolio experience matches Edgewater's blend of housing, retail, and workplace, and their local reputation strengthens the district's standing with Salem stakeholders, lenders, and the community.

Development Leadership & Advisory · Lake Oswego, OR

Macree Companies

macreeco.com · Founded 2022 · Kyle Zaylor, Founder & Managing Principal

Macree is a real estate investment and development firm focused on multifamily, mixed-use, and hospitality projects across the Pacific Northwest and Mountain West. The firm works across the capital stack: leading projects as developer and GP, investing alongside aligned partners, and advising sponsors on entitlement, project strategy, and institutional-grade investment committee support. Founder Kyle Zaylor has designed, entitled, and constructed over 5,000 multifamily units, 350,000 SF of retail, and 1.7M SF of office, totaling more than $3 billion in project value, and holds an MBA and MS in Architecture from Washington University in St. Louis.

Capabilities

Ground-up and adaptive reuse development, direct and co-investment, and strategic advisory. Particular strength at the intersection of design, entitlement, and capital, with a stated thesis of finding value in complex sites that require more than a good pro forma.

Potential Value to Edgewater

An extension-of-team development leader for early phases: owner's representation, entitlement strategy, design management, and delivery. Macree's integration model fits the master developer structure, letting Now City hold vision and standards while Macree drives execution. Their appetite for complex, place-making sites aligns directly with the Edgewater thesis.

Full-Service Platform · Portland, OR

Ethos Companies

ethoscompanies.com · Three affiliated firms serving the Pacific Northwest

Ethos Companies is a full-service real estate platform built from three firms that operate independently and partner seamlessly. Ethos Development is a values-based multifamily investor and developer (ground-up and value-add) and a 1% for the Planet member, combining affordability, energy efficiency, and creative housing solutions. Meritus Property Group provides property and asset management across multifamily, co-living, retail, office, and industrial, with specific expertise in lease-ups of newly delivered multifamily. Ethos Commercial Advisors handles tenant representation, agency leasing, investment sales, and debt and equity placement nationally, with offices in Oregon, Washington, and Texas.

Capabilities

Development, asset management, property management, commercial brokerage, and capital advisory under one roof. Operates retail, commercial, industrial, and multifamily assets across the Portland and Seattle metros.

Potential Value to Edgewater

The operating backbone of the district: lease-up execution for new residential phases, ground-floor retail leasing and merchandising, and long-term property and asset management that protects district-level outcomes. Their values orientation (affordability, energy efficiency, environmental commitment) matches the Edgewater brand, and their capital advisory arm adds a second channel to debt and equity.

Capital Advisory · National

Ludian Advisors

ludianadvisors.com · Beth Mercante, Founder & CEO · $2B+ closed

Ludian is an award-winning capital advisory firm that structures and closes complex real estate financings from $20M to $200M and beyond, spanning ground-up construction, mixed-use redevelopment, and complicated acquisitions. Founder Beth Mercante brings a development and finance background spanning KPMG, the Quantum Fund, and AIG Global Real Estate, and won REBNY's Most Ingenious Deal of the Year. The bench includes managing directors with deep lending, institutional asset management (Prudential PRISA II), and capital formation experience. Recognitions include 2023 Texas Mortgage Broker of the Year and Connect CRE Top Mortgage Broker for arranging over $500M across 15 complex financings.

Capabilities

Senior debt placement, subordinate financing (preferred equity and mezzanine), JV and Co-GP equity through institutional and family office relationships, and credit enhancement advisory. They structure capital rather than simply source it, and in select cases convert success fees to equity participation for alignment.

Potential Value to Edgewater

The capital stack architect for Phase 1 and beyond: construction debt at competitive leverage, preferred or mezzanine capital that preserves sponsor control, and Co-GP equity relationships suited to a multi-phase district. Their closed work includes a $47M mixed-use construction loan in Seattle with note-on-note and credit enhancement, a direct comp for the structures Edgewater will use.
