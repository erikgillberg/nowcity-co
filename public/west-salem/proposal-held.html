Cover Letter

January 14, 2026 · To Erik Gillberg, Ritchie Ju, and Trent Michels

Held Architecture is excited to submit this proposal to serve as the Owner’s Design Consultant (ODC) for the transformative Now City West Salem District, a 5.29-acre Phase 1 mixed-use innovation neighborhood that sets the foundation for a larger regenerative urban ecosystem in Salem, Oregon. We share Now City’s bold vision of a tech-forward, wellness-oriented district where housing, innovation infrastructure (including the AI Innovation Hub), nature, mobility, and community life integrate seamlessly, leveraging the site’s Opportunity Zone advantages and rezoning momentum.

With proven experience navigating entitlements and collaborating with Salem’s zoning department, most notably through successful delivery of entitlements for The Cannery mixed-use riverfront project on the Willamette River, we have reassembled the same seamless multidisciplinary team that delivered that project. This coalition of leading specialists is uniquely aligned with your ten core design principles, particularly Human Wellness & Quality of Life, Ecological Regeneration, Low-Carbon High-Performance Buildings, Open Space as Organizing Framework, and Resilience & Preparedness.

This coalition brings proven Oregon expertise in regenerative, low-carbon projects (mass-timber systemization, all-electric systems, net-zero strategies, and experiential third-places), ensuring robust Owner’s Project Requirements with prescriptive parametric criteria, design intent illustrations, and guardrails to protect quality, budget, and innovation through the hybrid delivery process. We fully embrace the ODC’s independent oversight role, setting baselines, reviewing compliance, and minimizing change-order risks, while delivering continuity via enhanced BIM turnover, early engagement workflows, and value engineering aligned with your long-term vision for up to 40 acres, mobility innovation, and an I-5-visible landmark.

All team members are licensed in Oregon, and we are committed to cost and schedule discipline, phased future-proofing, open building, and assembling a high-power local coalition to catalyze this district’s regional impact. We look forward to the opportunity to partner with Now City to redefine Salem’s urban identity and create a landmark regenerative district that attracts OSU-affiliated talent while uplifting the broader community.

Kristina Held, AIA, LEED AP BD+C, CPHCPresident, HELD Architecture, PC · kristina@heldarch.com · Oregon license ARF-14718

Approach

Aligned With Now City’s Ten Core Principles

Held, with IMEG, AKS, and Lango Hansen, approaches West Salem as a regenerative, tech-forward urban ecosystem: a 5.29-acre Phase 1 that scales thoughtfully to 20+ acres. Drawing on the market feasibility work, the team emphasizes inclusive, high-performance spaces aligned with MU-III zoning and Opportunity Zone incentives.

Entitlement-first sequencing. The team’s key lesson from delivering Salem entitlements: civil design must run nearly to design development to secure city approval, while architecture and engineering advance to a 50% DD handoff for design-build procurement. Owner’s Project Requirements carry design intent illustrations, performance specifications, and parametric criteria equivalent to 50-60% design development, so quality survives the handoff.

Cost discipline throughout. Independent estimates at schematic design and OPR milestones, value engineering to hold budget, and OPR enforcement to minimize change orders. Performance strategies (Passive House envelopes achieving up to 75% utility savings, daylight, ventilation, views to green space) are underwritten in the cost model rather than treated as aspirations.

Wellness woven in. Family-friendly amenities, accessible playgrounds, wellness trails, covered outdoor rooms as all-weather extensions of indoor living, and proximity to childcare and schools, supporting daily activity across ages.
