The Machine

Build. Fund. Operate. Repeat.

Build
One standardized building chassis, factory-delivered by partners. Sweden runs this play at national scale: roughly 10,000 apartments, about five months faster, 20-25% below comparable cost.3
Fund
A public-private capital stack (TIF, HUD, C-PACE, OZ, mission capital) engineered to beat conventional financing costs, district by district.
Operate
We hold everything we build. Metered performance and forecast error feed back into the chassis and the underwriting, so every district lowers the cost of the next.

The Arc

AvalonBay took thirty years to reach 97,000 homes.6

That is the scale we aim at, and the honest math says a developer alone gets there only by becoming a REIT. So the company is built as three engines: own a few districts, put the product in everyone else's, and operate all of it. The engines are live in sequence, and the first is running now.

The Ask

Platform equity, pre-seed. It carries the first district through entitlement and capitalization, finishes the housing catalog and its factory framework, and makes the first operating hire. District capital is raised separately and ring-fenced. Terms in conversation.
