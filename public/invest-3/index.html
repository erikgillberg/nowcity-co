The Machine

Build. Fund. Operate. Repeat.

Build
One standardized building chassis, designed for factory delivery. Sweden runs this play at national scale: roughly 10,000 apartments, about five months faster, 20-25% below comparable cost.2
Fund
A public-private capital stack (TIF, HUD, C-PACE, OZ, mission capital), modeled below conventional financing costs, assembled district by district.
Operate
We hold everything we build. Metered performance and forecast error will feed back into the chassis and the underwriting. That is how each district lowers the cost of the next.
