The Danish District product effort · Salem, Oregon and beyond · 2026

Now City and a coalition of Danish designers, engineers, companies, and institutions are exploring a serious question: what would it take to make Salem, Oregon the most liveable city in America, using a new kind of urban district designed and engineered as an exportable product of Denmark, delivered by an American master developer, and repeatable across the many cities that need it.

Signing this document expresses interest and support for exploring that idea seriously. It creates no obligation, no exclusivity, and no commitment of funds. It says: this deserves formation, and I want a hand in shaping the part I know best.
