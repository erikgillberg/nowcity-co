The Why · What People Pay For

The Signal

Physical AI just raised $1.7B to automate atoms.6 Neighborhoods are where it lands.

In July 2026, Atoms, led by Andreessen Horowitz, was capitalized to digitize and operate the physical world: food, mining, transport. That bits-to-atoms arc is coming to the built world, and it needs a deployment layer: real places with one owner, instrumented from day one. Whoever owns that layer becomes essential to everyone building the machines. Now City is built physical-AI native: instrumented from day one, with open integration points ready for systems like the ones Atoms is building.
